# 🏠 InvestorLens Home

Welcome to **InvestorLens** — a knowledge vault for viewing companies, sectors, and deals
through a consistent investment lens. This note is the map of content; everything branches
from here.

## Maps of Content
- [[Companies/_Index|📇 Companies]] — one note per company you track
- [[Sectors/_Index|🏭 Sectors]] — sector overviews and dynamics
- [[Theses/_Index|💡 Investment Theses]] — the ideas that tie companies together
- [[People/_Index|🧑‍💼 People & Contacts]] — founders, operators, and relationships

## Frameworks
- [[Frameworks/Investment Checklist|✅ Investment Checklist]] — the questions to answer before committing
- [[Frameworks/Scoring Rubric|📊 Scoring Rubric]] — score opportunities on a common scale

## Templates
- [[Templates/Company Profile]]
- [[Templates/Investment Memo]]
- [[Templates/Meeting Note]]

## Workflow
1. **Capture** — drop raw ideas, tickers, and links into [[Inbox/Welcome|📥 Inbox]].
2. **Profile** — create a company note from [[Templates/Company Profile]].
3. **Assess** — score it with the [[Frameworks/Scoring Rubric]] and run the [[Frameworks/Investment Checklist]].
4. **Decide** — write up conviction as an [[Templates/Investment Memo]] under the relevant [[Theses/_Index|thesis]].
5. **Track** — log conversations with [[Templates/Meeting Note]] and link them back to the company.

> [!tip] Getting started
> Open the command palette and run **Templates: Insert template** to spin up a new note
> from any of the templates above. The Templates folder is already configured.
