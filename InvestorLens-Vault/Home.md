# 🏛️ InvestorLens Vault

A knowledge base for investor research, deal flow, and investment decisions — part of the **AXIOM-CODEX** system.

## 🗂️ Map of Content

### People & Firms
- [[Investors]] — individual investors, angels, and operators
- [[Firms]] — VC firms, funds, and syndicates

### Deals & Analysis
- [[Theses]] — investment theses and focus areas
- [[Due Diligence]] — deal evaluations and memos
- [[Portfolio]] — companies you track or hold
- [[Meetings]] — call and meeting notes

### Reference
- [[Research]] — market and sector research
- [[Resources]] — frameworks and reference material

## ✍️ Quick Start
1. Drop raw notes into **00-Inbox**, then refile them into the right section.
2. Create structured notes from **Templates** — `Cmd/Ctrl + P → Insert template`.
3. Link liberally with `[[wikilinks]]` and tag with `#investor`, `#firm`, `#thesis`, etc.
4. (Optional) Install the **Dataview** community plugin to activate the auto-lists on each section index.
