# 💡 Investment Theses

A thesis is a falsifiable statement about how the world is changing and how to profit from it.
It ties together the [[Companies/_Index|companies]] and [[Sectors/_Index|sectors]] that express it.

## Active theses
- _No theses yet. State each one as: "Because **X** is happening, **Y** kind of company will
  win, and the market underappreciates it because **Z**."_

## Anatomy of a good thesis
- **Claim** — the one-sentence bet.
- **Evidence** — data and observations supporting it.
- **Disconfirmers** — what would prove it wrong (watch these).
- **Expression** — which companies best capture the upside.
- **Time horizon** — when you expect it to play out.
