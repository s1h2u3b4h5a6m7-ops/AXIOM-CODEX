# ✅ Investment Checklist

Run every opportunity through these questions before committing. The goal is not a perfect
score — it is to make sure nothing important went unexamined.

## Business
- [ ] What does the company actually sell, and to whom?
- [ ] Why does the customer buy — and why now?
- [ ] How does it make money, and how do unit economics scale?

## Moat
- [ ] What is the durable competitive advantage (network, cost, brand, switching costs, IP)?
- [ ] Is the moat widening or narrowing?

## Market
- [ ] How large is the addressable market, and how fast is it growing?
- [ ] What is the realistic share this company can win?

## People
- [ ] Is the team capable and aligned (incentives, ownership, track record)?
- [ ] How do they behave when things go wrong?

## Financials
- [ ] Growth, margins, cash generation, and balance-sheet health?
- [ ] What does the valuation imply must be true to earn a return?

## Risk
- [ ] What are the top three ways this loses money?
- [ ] What would make me exit, and what is the downside if I am wrong?

## Decision
- [ ] Score with the [[Frameworks/Scoring Rubric]].
- [ ] Write the [[Templates/Investment Memo]] and state the one-line reason to own it.
